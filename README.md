SOLMARA HEAT is a fully original 3D open-world crime game set in the fictional city of Solmara, built from scratch for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. You play Marco Solis, a driver pulled back into the streets by fixer Lena Cruz, working through five story jobs — stealing rides, outrunning the law, settling scores with a rival crew, and finally hitting the Solmara Reserve Bank. The city stays alive around you: traffic flows down every avenue, pedestrians cross the sidewalks, and the police respond with a wanted level from 0 to 5 whenever you stir up trouble. Everything runs offline in a desktop browser — no network requests, no downloads — and progress saves automatically so you can pick up right where you left off.

## Controls

- **W / A / S / D** or **arrow keys** — walk and steer; hold **Shift** to sprint on foot
- **Mouse drag** — orbit the camera around Marco
- **E** — get in or out of a car (walk up to any parked car to take it)
- **Left click** — fire the pistol
- **F** — punch at close range
- **Space** — jump
- **P** or **Esc** — pause the game
- **M** — mute / unmute the sound

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
